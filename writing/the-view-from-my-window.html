# The View From My Window *By Lenin Brey Ballog* i saw a bird find a branch up high. it was bare, and the wind was cold. she tried to build a nest there. she carried heavy twigs in her beak. her wings grew sore. she wanted a home with a view of the whole sky, but the rain kept coming down. every time it rained, she took shelter beneath a fallen log that kept her dry. through the days she worked to build a nest, it was taking too long. so she dropped her twigs. she flew away. she flew down to an old fallen trunk, half-buried in the earth, its insides hollowed by time. it was already hollow. it did not need work. it did not ask anything from her. she sits at the entrance where the other birds can see her. she preens her feathers. she sings a loud, bright song. she tells the forest her dark little hole is a paradise. she pretends it is warm inside. she pretends she does not see the decay. at night, she turns her back to the open sky and tucks her head beneath her wing. i wonder if she ever thinks about that high branch in the storm. i wonder if she knows what could have been built there if she hadn't given up so soon. whatever. she has her hollow log now. she tells herself she is safe.
